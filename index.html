Pair your Bamboo Folio

Do this once. It gives the Folio a private key so only this app can read it.

  1. Turn on Bluetooth and Location on your phone.
  2. Hold the Folio's round button for about 6 seconds, until the light blinks blue .
  3. Tap Pair Folio and pick your Folio from the list. When asked, press the button once more.

Your Folio is paired

No need to pair again. Save a page on the Folio with its button, then tap Sync. Pages are copied here and cleared from the Folio's memory.
